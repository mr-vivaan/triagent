# Orders and delivery policy
- Orders can be changed or cancelled free of charge until they are marked as shipped.
- Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days; express delivery takes 1-2 business days. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.
- The shipping address can be changed before shipment from My Account > Orders > Edit address. After shipment the carrier must be contacted.
- Order tracking is available with the tracking number emailed once the order ships.
- If an order has not arrived 7 business days after shipping, support will open an investigation with the carrier.
