# Billing policy
- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for unused products. Refunds are returned to the original payment method in 5-7 business days after approval.
- Duplicate charges are investigated by the billing team; the customer should share the order number and the dates of both charges.
- Invoices can be downloaded from My Account > Billing > Invoices. A copy can also be emailed on request.
- Cancellation fees apply only to orders already shipped; orders not yet shipped can be cancelled free of charge.
- Accepted payment methods are credit/debit cards, PayPal and bank transfer. Failed payments can be retried from My Account > Billing.
