# Account and subscription policy
- Passwords can be reset using the Forgot password link on the login page; the reset link is valid for 24 hours.
- To change email address or profile details go to My Account > Profile.
- Accounts can be created on the sign-up page with an email address; email verification is required.
- Subscriptions can be cancelled any time from My Account > Subscriptions and stay active until the end of the paid period.
- The newsletter can be switched on or off in My Account > Preferences.
